## Version 1.1

*(1,897 characters)*

```
THE DEEP JUST GOT DEEPER! ⛏️

4 NEW BIOMES
Dig past the Abyss into the Ancient Ruins, the Frozen Depths, the Spore Hollow and finally The Void. Each one has new ore, new creatures and its own soundtrack.

4 NEW BOSSES
Face the Stone Pharaoh, the Frost Wyrm, the Sporemother and the Star Eater, each with three deadly powers.

8 NEW CREATURES
Scarabs, Tomb Wraiths, Frost Wisps, Ice Urchins, Spore Puffs, Bog Toads, Singularities and Void Imps.

NEW HAZARDS
Chill slows you down, Poison drains your fuel, and Gravity pulls you in. Find the boss parts that make you immune.

LEGENDARY PARTS & RARITIES
Every boss win now earns a core. Collect 10 from a boss and its Legendary part goes on sale in the Garage. Garage parts now come in five rarities, from Common to Legendary, with 4 new Legendary parts to chase.

6 NEW PERKS
Bounty Hunter, Daredevil, Rock Breaker, Boss Breaker, Auto-Repair and Thermal Core: 19 perks in total.

FIELD GUIDE: PERKS
A new Perks tab in the Field Guide collects every perk you discover. The guide now has 30 creature pages too.

SOUND & MUSIC
Retro sound effects and a chiptune theme for every biome and boss fight. Turn them on or off in Settings.

iCLOUD SAVE
Your progress now follows you to your other devices and survives reinstalling.

HOW TO PLAY
A new guide (the ? button) explains every rule, hazard and biome.

REPORT A BUG
Found a problem? Settings → Report a bug sends us an email with the details we need.

BALANCE
• Fuel upgrades now give +10 per level
• Reserve Tank: refills your tank, +5 capacity
• Garage parts and part upgrades cost more
• Spiky geodes look cracked when your Diamond Bit can break them

FIXES
• Drilling a spiky geode with the Diamond Bit no longer empties your fuel tank
• "End Run" now works before you start drilling and during a perk choice
• Fixed a boss banner that said "Guardian of the The Abyss"

Thanks for digging with us!
```

### Notes for the release
- **Fuel change:** players with Fuel upgrades get a smaller tank after updating (each level is now +10 instead of +22). Their levels and spent gold are kept.
- **Boss parts:** parts players already own from 1.0 stay owned. New ones must be bought after 10 cores.
- **If 1.0.1 ships first** with the fixes below, delete the first two lines under FIXES from the 1.1 notes.
- **Store page for 1.1:** the description, keywords text and screenshots still say 6 biomes, 6 bosses, 18 creatures and 13 perks. Update them to 10 biomes, 10 bosses, 30 creatures and 19 perks.
