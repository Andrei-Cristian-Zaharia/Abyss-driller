## Description (4000 max)
```
Hold to drill. Steer anywhere. Dig as deep as you dare.

Abyss Driller is a fast, hand-drawn arcade digging game. Bore through rock, scoop up glittering ore and battle giant bosses on your way down to the Abyss. Every run is a fresh shaft, and every run you'll get a little deeper.

DIG IN ANY DIRECTION
Drag to steer your drill anywhere: around boulders, back up your own tunnel, or straight into a monster's face. Ram critters with your bit, slip through tight gaps and chain near misses into combos.

BEAT 6 GIANT BOSSES
Every biome ends in a boss fight with a cinematic entrance. The Worm Queen, Bat Matriarch, Beetle Tank, Crystal Golem, Magma Titan and the Abyss Angler each hit with three deadly powers, and get angrier at half health.

STRIKE IT RICH
Copper, silver, gold, platinum, mythril and pearl: the deeper you dig, the more your ore is worth. Hit a Mother Lode and every nugget pays double. Fill your hold, trade up for richer ore and cash it all in when the run ends.

BUILD YOUR DREAM DRILL
Mix and match bits, hulls, tracks and modules in the Garage, then upgrade them all the way to Mk III. 20 parts to unlock, and every boss drops a legendary part of its own, from Queen's Fang to the Abyss Lantern.

EVERY RUN IS DIFFERENT
Choose 1 of 3 perks as you go, stack them up and build combos to trigger OVERDRIVE, when your drill smashes through everything in its way. Survive cave-ins, gold rushes and sudden lights-out darkness.

EXPLORE 6 DEADLY BIOMES
Dig from the Surface through the Shallow Caves, Deep Caves, Crystal Caves and the Magma Core, all the way down to the glowing Abyss. Each biome has its own creatures, hazards and treasure.

DISCOVER 18 CREATURES
Worms, moles, cave spiders, prism slimes, fire slugs, anglers and more. Meet them all to fill your Field Guide, complete with tips for beating each one.

DAILY CHALLENGE
The same shaft for every player, every day. Same rig, same rocks, same bosses. How far can you get?

WHY YOU'LL KEEP DIGGING
• Quick runs, perfect for a short break
• Workshop upgrades with no level cap
• Simple one-thumb controls with adjustable sensitivity
• Charming hand-inked art
• Ads never interrupt a run, and Remove Ads turns them all off
• Play anywhere, no account needed

Grab your drill. The Abyss is waiting.
```
